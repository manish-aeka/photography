About Anupam Dutta

Wildlife & bird photographer, Kolkata, India

Anupam Dutta is a passionate wildlife photographer, with a special love for birds and their habitats. His photography journeys span India and international wildlife destinations, with a focus on rare and lesser-known species. His work has been published in wildlife, travel and photography magazines.

“With every click, I freeze emotions that deserve to be remembered.”
